Source: Gallup, "Increase Productivity at the Lowest Possible Cost."

"Replacing an employee costs between one half and two times their annual salary."

Source: Gallup, "This Fixable Problem Costs U.S. Businesses $1 Trillion." Gallup's worked example on the same page: $660,000 to $2.6 million a year for a 100 person company at a $50,000 average salary.

Read it at Gallup

Verified, primary source, Nov 9, 2026

"Managers account for 70 percent of the variance in team engagement."

Source: Gallup, research on managers and team engagement. Note what this means: variance in team engagement scores. It does not mean managers cause 70 percent of disengagement.

Read it at Gallup

Verified, primary source, Nov 24, 2026

"42 percent of people who quit voluntarily say their manager or organization could have done something to prevent it."

Source: Gallup, "42% of Employee Turnover Is Preventable but Often Ignored," study of 717 voluntary leavers. This is what leavers reported about their own exits.

Read it at Gallup

Verified, primary source, Nov 24, 2026

"US employee engagement fell below 30 percent for the first time in 15 years, at 29 percent."

Source: Gallup, Q3 2026 engagement reading, reported October 28, 2026, with 18 percent actively disengaged. This supersedes the older 31 percent figure from 2024 data.

Read it at Gallup

Verified, primary source, Nov 9, 2026

"Low engagement costs the world economy 8.9 trillion dollars."

Source: Gallup, State of the Global Workplace report. This is the global figure.

Read the report page at Gallup
